How it worked

From review to reply in three steps

01

Connect Google

Owners signed in with Google OAuth and picked their Business Profile. Reviews synced on connect and on a schedule after that.

02

AI drafts the reply

Each review went to Claude with the business name, rating and chosen tone, under guardrails: no invented facts, no promises, no competitor names.

03

Approve or auto-send

Owners edited and approved drafts from the dashboard, or switched on auto-reply. Replies posted straight back to Google.
